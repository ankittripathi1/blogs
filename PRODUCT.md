# Daylog

A personal "today I learned" blog by one developer. They write short notes about what they learned while building software, such as why `app.disable("x-powered-by")` matters in their UMS project.

## Users

- The author writes and publishes from a small admin area, often right after solving a problem.
- Readers are developers who arrive from a link or search and want the lesson fast.

## Product truth

- Each post is one Markdown file with simple headers: `#TITLE:` and `#DESCRIPTION:` are the main ones.
- Optional headers: `#PROJECT:`, `#DATE:`, `#TAGS:`, `#SNIPPET:`, `#COVER:`, `#FEATURED:`.
- Publishing commits the file to GitHub. GitHub Pages rebuilds the site.

## Brand commitments

- Low text. No marketing copy. The posts are the content.
- Simple over clever. Code must be easy to read.
- The old dark twilight reference is dropped.
